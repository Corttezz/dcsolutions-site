## Users

- People who reach dcsolutions.app from a product site, a search or a contact, and want to know who is behind IQNorth, Fytform, H2HScout and the other products. *(inferred)*
- Possible partners or contacts who want to get in touch with the company (email only). *(inferred)*

## Product Purpose

dcsolutions.app is the company website and product showcase of DC SOLUTIONS LTDA (Brazil). It presents the company and each product, and sends visitors to the product's own website. Nothing is sold on this site. Success: a visitor understands what each product is and reaches its site, or writes to contato@dcsolutions.app. *(inferred)*

## Positioning

Small products, each made to solve one thing, built and run end to end by the same team (code to customer support). Honest numbers: uncertainty is shown (confidence range in IQNorth, small-sample warning in H2HScout).

## Operating Context

- Static HTML/CSS served by a Cloudflare Worker (`dcsolutions-site`) with static assets; build `node ferramentas/build.mjs`; CSP by hash, no inline styles, no third-party scripts, fonts, analytics or cookies.
- The live page is selected by flags in `wrangler.jsonc`: `VITRINE_V4="1"` serves the English-only v4 at `/`, `/privacy`, `/terms` and the 404; `"0"` rolls back to v3.
- Each product card links to the product's own website; product texts come from each product's Lead.

## Product Principles

1. One problem per product; present each product plainly, with its real screens.
2. Honest numbers and claims: only what each product publicly states.
3. Whoever builds it runs it: the company is small and says so.
4. Privacy by default: no cookies, no tracking on this site.
