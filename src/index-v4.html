How we work

Four rules for every product that comes out of here.

  • One problem per product

    Each product exists to solve one thing. We would rather do that very well than do ten things halfway.

  • Honest numbers

    When a result is uncertain, we show it: a confidence range in IQNorth, a small-sample warning in H2HScout. In Fytform, you can look inside a sample plan before building yours.

  • Whoever builds it, runs it

    Product, design, code, infrastructure and support stay under one roof. Whoever made it is who answers when something goes wrong.

  • Privacy by default

    We collect only what the product needs to work. This site uses no cookies and no tracking tools.
