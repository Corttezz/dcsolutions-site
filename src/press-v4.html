Who makes IQNorth, Fytform, H2HScout, PartiuGringa and Runemoire?

IQNorth, Fytform, H2HScout, PartiuGringa and Runemoire are made by DC Solutions (DC SOLUTIONS LTDA), a digital products company from Brazil. DC Solutions builds and runs each product itself, including customer support.

Short description (for quoting)

DC Solutions is a Brazilian digital products company. It builds small products that each solve one problem: IQNorth (online IQ test), Fytform (workout and diet plans), H2HScout (Brasileirão statistics), PartiuGringa (international jobs for Brazilian developers) and Runemoire (a PC card game in development).

Product sheets

IQNorth

  • What it is: an online IQ test with 40 reasoning questions in 6 areas, no time limit per question, in about 20 minutes. The result is an estimated IQ with a 95% confidence range and a result by area.
  • Status: live · Website: https://iqnorth.com/en/ · Support: contato@iqnorth.com
  • Quote: “IQNorth is an online reasoning test made by DC Solutions, Brazil.”

Fytform

  • What it is: answer 17 questions in about 5 minutes and get the week’s workouts and a diet in grams, with food swaps, on your phone.
  • Status: live · Website: https://fytform.com/ · Support: contato@fytform.com
  • Quote: “Fytform builds workout and diet plans around each person. It is made by DC Solutions, Brazil.”

H2HScout

  • What it is: statistical analysis of the Brasileirão Série A, with each number adjusted for the strength of the opponent: 20 clubs and 38 rounds, with new data every round. When the sample is too small to trust, the screen says so.
  • Status: closed beta · Website: https://h2hscout.com/
  • Quote: “H2HScout is a statistical analysis of the Brasileirão made by DC Solutions, Brazil.”

PartiuGringa

  • What it is: helps Brazilian developers land international jobs: an AI review of the LinkedIn profile with a score from 0 to 100, remote roles open to Latin America and a tailored CV for each job.
  • Status: live · Website: https://partiugringa.com/
  • Quote: “PartiuGringa helps Brazilian developers get international jobs. It is made by DC Solutions, Brazil.”

Runemoire

  • What it is: a roguelike deckbuilder in pixel art for PC: 3 classes, 145 animated cards, jokers that change every play and 6 acts with a boss at the end of each one.
  • Status: in development · Website: https://runemoire.com/
  • Quote: “Runemoire is a roguelike deckbuilder for PC in development at DC Solutions, Brazil.”
