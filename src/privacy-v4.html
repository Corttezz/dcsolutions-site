Who we are

This website belongs to DC SOLUTIONS LTDA, Brazilian company registration (CNPJ) 63.680.570/0001-90, whose registered address is shown at the bottom of this page. We are the controller of the data processed on this website.

What this website collects

  • No cookies. We use no cookies, no advertising pixels, no analytics tools and no other trackers.
  • Technical logs. The website is served by Cloudflare, which processes the IP address and technical data of each request to deliver the page and protect it against abuse. We do not use these logs to identify visitors.
  • Emails you send us. If you write to contato@dcsolutions.app, we use your address and the content of the message only to reply. The message is received by Cloudflare’s email service and delivered to our mailbox.

Our products

IQNorth, Fytform, H2HScout, PartiuGringa and Runemoire have their own accounts, payments and data, handled under the privacy policy published on each product’s website. This page covers only the company website.

Legal basis and retention

We reply to messages based on our legitimate interest and, where applicable, to fulfil a request from you (article 7, items V and IX, of Brazil’s General Data Protection Law, the LGPD). We keep the conversation while it is useful for the reply or for as long as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction, anonymization or deletion of your data, and for information about who it was shared with (article 18 of the LGPD). Write to contato@dcsolutions.app. If you are not satisfied, you can contact Brazil’s National Data Protection Authority (ANPD).

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes too.
